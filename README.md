## 🔬 Featured Bioinformatic Project

### 📌 [TP53 Transcriptomic & Survival Analysis in Breast Invasive Carcinoma (TCGA-BRCA)](https://github.com/Preetika-Nanda/tp53-breast-cancer-tcga-analysis)
* **Multi-Omics Integration:** Analyzed differential RNA-seq expression ($n=1,376$), Kaplan-Meier overall survival outcomes ($n=1,070$), and somatic driver mutation landscapes across TCGA-BRCA and GTEx datasets using GEPIA2 and cBioPortal.
* **Key Quantitative Discoveries:** 
  * Demonstrated a **1.62-fold upregulation** ($p < 0.01$) of *TP53* mRNA transcript expression in breast adenocarcinoma tissue relative to solid normal controls.
  * Derived a **40% increased hazard of mortality** ($\text{HR} = 1.40, p = 0.038$) in high *TP53* expression cohorts, linking transcript accumulation to missense mutation stabilization.
